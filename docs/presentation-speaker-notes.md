### Slide 1 — The Problem
**Slide Title:** The Campus Sustainability Blind Spot  
**Subtitle:** Why higher education institutions struggle to achieve net-zero targets.

**Speaker Script:**
> "Good morning, judges. College campuses operate like miniature cities—consuming gigawatt-hours of power, millions of litres of water, and generating tons of solid waste every year.
> 
> Yet campus sustainability management today suffers from three fundamental bottlenecks:
> 1. **Data Fragmentation:** Energy meters, utility invoices, and facility maintenance reports live in isolated silos.
> 2. **Physical Blind Spots:** Critical incidents like overflowing waste bins or exterior water pipe leaks are completely invisible to digital systems until someone complains.
> 3. **Reactive Decision-Making:** Sustainability committees are forced to make multimillion-dollar policy and retrofit decisions based on guesswork, with no safe sandbox to test interventions beforehand.
>
> We need a connected system that bridges the gap between physical campus operations and predictive sustainability intelligence."

### Slide 2 — Our Solution
**Slide Title:** EcoTwin AI: Intelligent Campus Digital Twin  
**Subtitle:** See the problem. Simulate the solution. Make the campus sustainable.

**Speaker Script:**
> "Introducing **EcoTwin AI**—an integrated campus intelligence platform that unites Computer Vision, facility telemetry, a 2.5D spatial Digital Twin, deterministic simulations, and Generative AI.
> 
> Rather than a static analytics dashboard with a disconnected chatbot, EcoTwin AI is an interconnected workflow. It maintains an active spatial model of campus facilities—such as the Academic Block, Library, and Student Residence.
> 
> Most importantly, our core constitutional principle is **data integrity**:
> We strictly distinguish measured sensor facts from simulated projections, Computer Vision observations, and AI-generated explanations. Projected savings are never presented as measured facts."

---

### Slide 3 — How It Works
**Slide Title:** The Connected Architecture  
**Subtitle:** From raw field telemetry to grounded executive action.

**Speaker Script:**
> "Let’s look under the hood at how data flows through EcoTwin AI:
> 1. **Sensor & Facility Telemetry:** Hourly energy (kWh), water volume (L), and waste fill levels are ingested through validated schemas with strict physical unit discipline.
> 2. **Dedicated Computer Vision:** When campus staff upload imagery, our dedicated CV pipeline performs magic byte inspection, detects anomalies like waste accumulation or leaks, and registers them in a `requires_review` state for human validation.
> 3. **The Digital Twin Core:** A unified virtual representation aggregating spatial coordinates, real-time telemetry, and confirmed incidents.
> 4. **Deterministic Impact Engine:** When testing an intervention, backend mathematical formulas—not stochastic AI—calculate exact kilowatt-hour and dollar impacts.
> 5. **xAI Grok Intelligence:** Grok is queried with structured grounding context—citing verified building data and simulation numbers verbatim to generate executive action plans and audit reports."

### Slide 4 — Our Innovation
**Slide Title:** The Sustainability Time Machine  
**Subtitle:** Deterministic policy forecasting before capital allocation.

**Speaker Script:**
> "Our signature innovation is the **Sustainability Time Machine**.
> 
> In conventional tools, administrators debate hypothetical interventions blindly. With the Time Machine, decision-makers select a facility, choose a resource—Energy, Water, or Waste—and configure actionable interventions like operating hours curtailment, LED retrofits, or waste segregation.
> 
> The backend runs transparent, reproducible engineering formulas and returns:
> - **Comparative Impact:** Bar charts contrasting verified baseline vs projected consumption.
> - **30-Day Trajectory:** Cumulative savings curves forecasting monthly ROI.
> - **Engineering Assumptions:** Full transparency into commercial utility tariffs and mathematical factors.
> 
> Identical inputs yield identical results every time. It gives campus leadership the evidence-backed confidence needed before signing off on operational changes."
