The Challenge
The Campus Sustainability Blind Spot
Why higher education institutions struggle to achieve net-zero commitments
01
Data Fragmentation
Utility bills, electric meters, and maintenance logs live in disconnected silos. Facility teams lack unified spatial context to identify resource anomalies.
02
Physical Blind Spots
Critical incidents like overflowing waste bins, equipment faults, or exterior water leaks remain invisible to digital systems until someone files a complaint.
03
Reactive Guesswork
Sustainability committees make major policy and retrofit decisions based on guesswork, lacking safe, deterministic sandboxes to test interventions beforehand.

Architecture
The Connected End-to-End Pipeline
From raw field observation to verifiable executive intervention
01. Telemetry Ingestion
Simulated and manual readings (kWh, L, %) ingested with unit discipline and provenance labeling.
02. Computer Vision

05. Grok Grounding
xAI Grok cites verified facility metrics and simulation numbers to produce grounded audit reports.

Slide 5 / 6
Prototype Evidence
Working Product & Technical Verification
Honest audit from our Phase 5 and Phase 6 automated test runs
VERIFIED LIVE CAPABILITIES (32/32 TESTS PASS)
  • 2.5D Isometric SVG Campus Twin: Selectable polygons for ACADEMIC, LIB, RESIDENCE with active CV observation markers.
  • Computer Vision Pipeline: Validates magic bytes (JPEG/PNG/WebP), enforces 8MB limit, and requires human review.
  • Sustainability Time Machine: Multi-tab comparative analytics, bound checks, and reproducible engineering models.
  • Grok AI Advisor & Reports: Structured grounding, 0-10 priority scoring, and instant PDF audit reports.
  • Production Hardened: 0 leaked secrets, clean 500 error sanitization, and Vite SPA client-side deep routing redirects.
DOCUMENTED BOUNDARIES & DATA HONESTY
  • Telemetry Persistence Ownership: Backend routes for /api/campus, /api/buildings, and /api/sensors belong to Person 2.
  • Zero Fabricated Data: When pending endpoints return 404, the frontend displays transparent <DependencyNotice> cards rather than faking telemetry.
  • Unit Discipline Strictly Enforced: Energy in kWh, Water volume in L, and Waste in kg/%. Instantaneous kW and volumes are never mixed.
  • Provider Fault Tolerance: If XAI_API_KEY is unconfigured, the system safely falls back to deterministic audit calculations.
  • Deployment Integrity: Tested against ephemeral container constraints; persistent disk required for production SQLite.
